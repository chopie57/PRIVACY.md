Privacy Policy for BNBA Table Extractor
>
 Last updated: October 18, 2026
>
 This extension, "BNBA Table Extractor," is designed to help users extract data from HTML tables on the web and convert it into Excel files locally on their devices.
>
 Data Collection and Usage:
 - This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any user data.
 - All processing is performed locally on the user's browser.
 - The extension does not track user behavior, nor does it share any information with third parties.
>
 Permissions:
 - The extension requests "activeTab" and "scripting" permissions solely to perform its primary function: reading the table content on the page currently viewed by the user, upon their explicit click on the extension button.
>
 If you have any questions, you can contact the developer.
